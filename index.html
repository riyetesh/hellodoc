How a visit works

From an open time to a signed assessment

Four stages, nothing automatic. Choose a stage to see what happens in the product at that point.

Find care

The public directory lists doctors an administrator has approved. Each entry carries the specialty, the consultation fee and the current state of appointment availability, so the first decision is made on facts rather than a marketing page.

Open the directory

Choose a visit

Open times come from the doctor's own availability. Selecting one creates a booking and an entry in the patient account, and the same entry appears in the doctor's workspace. Nothing is booked until the patient confirms.

Attend the consultation

During the visit the doctor records the symptoms the patient reports and, separately, the findings they observe. Free text observations stay with the doctor and are never sent to the model.

Record the assessment

The doctor reviews the consultation information, writes their own assessment and plan, and saves the record against that appointment. A later correction is stored as a new revision, preserving the original assessment history.
