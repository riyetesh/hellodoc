What HelloDoc does

About HelloDoc

HelloDoc is a digital front door to everyday healthcare: a directory of verified doctors, a booking flow, and a consultation record your doctor writes during the visit.

Structured symptom capture

During a visit your doctor records what you report from a fixed symptom vocabulary, so the record stays consistent. Free text observations are stored for the doctor and never reach the model.

Appointments with verified doctors

Compare speciality, consultation fee and open slots before you book. Cancelling is in your hands if plans change, and every doctor in the directory has been approved by an administrator.

Doctor led assessment

The model offers candidate conditions as decision support. Your doctor reviews them against their own findings, records the assessment and plan, and can download the report as a PDF.

How it works

From a symptom to a signed assessment

Four steps, nothing automatic. An appointment is only booked when you choose a slot, and only your doctor can record clinical findings.

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Register as a patient, or apply as a doctor and wait for an administrator to verify your credentials.

  2. 2

    Choose a doctor and a slot

    The directory lists speciality, fee and open availability, so you can decide before you book.

  3. 3

    Attend the visit

    Your doctor records symptoms, reviews the model guidance and writes the clinical assessment.

  4. 4

    Keep the record

    The assessment, any later revision and the PDF report stay attached to that appointment.

The model suggests candidate conditions from reported symptoms. It is not a diagnosis, no calibrated confidence is shown, and every clinical decision belongs to the doctor.

Two sides, one record

Safety and privacy

How HelloDoc guards the record

These are properties of the current build, not certifications. An independent security and privacy review is still an open item.

Approved clinicians only

Doctor accounts start pending. Only an administrator can approve one, and every protected request checks the role, the approval and any suspension again before it answers.

Your identity stays out of the model

The model receives symptom identifiers only: no name, note or appointment detail. It runs as a private service behind a server side token.

Records that keep their history

A symptom list can be revised, but the original prediction is kept. The model output is frozen once stored, and the full lineage stays with the assessment.
