Record the assessment

The doctor reviews the experimental candidate conditions, writes their own assessment and plan, and saves the record against that appointment. A later correction is stored as a new revision, so the original model output is never rewritten.

Experimental model assistance

In a real consultation, the model receives only symptom identifiers and returns candidate conditions for the doctor to weigh against their own findings. It never sees a name, a note or an appointment detail, and it does not decide anything.

This demonstration never contacts the model and never shows a prediction. It exists to explain the separation of responsibilities.

Why HelloDoc exists

Technology should support the conversation, not replace it.

HelloDoc started with a question about access: what if the first step toward care were clearer for someone far from a hospital? The answer here is not a chatbot that diagnoses. It is a place where a patient's account of their symptoms and a clinician's own assessment meet in one record, with experimental model assistance used inside that workflow rather than on top of it.

Safety and privacy

How HelloDoc guards the record

These are properties of the current build, not certifications. An independent security and privacy review is still an open item.

Approved clinicians only
Doctor accounts start pending. Only an administrator can approve one, and every protected request checks the role, the approval and any suspension again before it answers.
Your identity stays out of the model
The model receives symptom identifiers only: no name, note or appointment detail. It runs as a private service behind a server side token.
Records that keep their history
A symptom list can be revised, but the original prediction is kept. The model output is frozen once stored, and the full lineage stays with the assessment.
