    Privacy and legal

    Registration stores your name, email and optional phone number. A booking stores the reason you type, and an assessment stores the symptoms and clinical notes a doctor records.

    Your information is used to sign you in, manage appointments and show a doctor the consultation you booked. It is not used to train the model.

    Supabase hosts authentication and the database. Apart from that processor and legal requirements, the prototype does not share your data.

    FAQs

    How do I create an account? Create an account by clicking the Sign Up button on the homepage. You can register with your email address.

    How can I search for a doctor? Use the search bar on the Find a doctor page to filter by doctor name, speciality or language. Search by symptom or location is not available.

    How do I book an appointment? Select a doctor from the results, choose a free time slot, and confirm the request.

    Can I check symptoms without an account? No. The public symptom checker has been withdrawn. The experimental model runs only inside the doctor assessment workflow for an appointment.

    Are results a diagnosis? No. The model returns one condition label with no calibrated confidence. It is decision support, and the doctor's own assessment is stored separately.

    How do I register as a doctor? Use Join as a doctor on the home page, choose the Doctor tab, and enter your professional details. An administrator reviews them before clinical tools unlock.

    What credentials are required? Specialty, registration or licence number, institution, years of experience and a written qualifications summary. No documents are uploaded in this prototype.

    How do I update my doctor profile? Resubmit professional details from the doctor dashboard while a review is pending or was rejected. An administrator updates the consultation fee and whether you are accepting appointments.
