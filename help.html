    FAQs

    How do I create an account? Create an account by clicking the Sign Up button on the homepage. You can register with your email address.

    How can I search for a doctor? Use the search bar on the homepage to look for doctors by symptom, location, or speciality.

    How do I book an appointment? Select a doctor from the search results, choose an available time slot, and confirm your appointment.

    How do I register as a doctor? Click on Register as a Doctor on the homepage. Provide your medical credentials and complete the verification process.

    What credentials are required? You need a valid medical license and proof of identity to register as an authorised doctor.

    How do I update my doctor profile? Use the profile settings in your dashboard to edit your speciality, experience, and hourly fee.
